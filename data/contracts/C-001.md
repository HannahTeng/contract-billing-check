# BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Agreement No. BESS-2026-001

This Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 14 March 2026 between **Northwind Energy Systems Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Supplier"), and **Helios Grid Storage LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("Customer").

## 1. Scope
Supplier shall design, manufacture, deliver and commission two (2) containerized battery energy storage units of 3.9 MWh nominal capacity each (the "Units"), together with the associated power conversion equipment and site controller software, at Customer's Pecos County site.

## 2. Contract Price
The total price for the Units and the services described in Section 1 is **USD 2,480,000.00** (the "Contract Price"), exclusive of applicable sales taxes. The Contract Price is fixed and shall not be adjusted except by a Change Order under Section 9.

## 3. Payment Schedule
Customer shall pay the Contract Price in the following installments:

| Milestone | Percentage of Contract Price | Invoiceable upon |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | 10% | execution of this Agreement |
| Shipment | 60% | ex-works shipment of both Units from Supplier's facility |
| Commissioning | 25% | successful completion of the commissioning test under Exhibit C |
| Final Acceptance | 5% | issuance of the Final Acceptance Certificate |

## 4. Payment Terms
Each invoice is due and payable **thirty (30) days** after the invoice date. Late payments bear interest at 1% per month.

## 5. Delivery
Supplier shall deliver both Units to the site no later than **30 September 2026** (the "Delivery Deadline").

## 6. Liquidated Damages
If Supplier fails to meet the Delivery Deadline for reasons other than Force Majeure or Customer-caused delay, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of **0.5% of the Contract Price for each full week of delay**, up to a maximum of **5% of the Contract Price**. Liquidated damages are Customer's sole remedy for late delivery.

## 7. Warranty
Supplier warrants the Units against defects in materials and workmanship for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** from Final Acceptance.

## 8. Order of Precedence
In the event of any conflict between the body of this Agreement and any Exhibit, the body of this Agreement shall prevail.

## 9. Change Orders
No change to the scope, price or schedule shall be effective unless set out in a written Change Order signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

## 10. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
