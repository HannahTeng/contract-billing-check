# FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT FOR SUPPLY OF ENERGY STORAGE CONTAINERS

Reference number: FA/2026/0912

This Framework Agreement is made between **Baltic Cell Systems OÜ** (the Supplier) and **Nordlicht Energie AG** (the Purchaser).

## Article 1 — Object
The Supplier undertakes to supply six (6) 20-foot energy storage containers, each fitted with lithium iron phosphate cells of 2.5 MWh usable capacity, together with thermal management and fire suppression, delivered DAP to the Purchaser's Hamburg logistics yard.

## Article 2 — Price
The aggregate price for all six containers amounts to **one million two hundred fifty thousand euros (EUR 1.25M)**, excluding value added tax.

## Article 3 — Terms of Payment
The Purchaser shall pay twenty percent (20%) of the aggregate price upon signature, fifty percent (50%) upon notification of readiness for dispatch, and thirty percent (30%) upon delivery. Invoices shall be settled within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date.

## Article 4 — Delivery
Delivery shall take place no later than the fifteenth day of October, two thousand and twenty-six (15 October 2026).

## Article 5 — Contractual Penalty
Should delivery be delayed, the Supplier shall pay a contractual penalty of zero point seven five percent (0.75%) of the aggregate price per commenced week of delay, limited to seven point five percent (7.5%) of the aggregate price in total.

## Article 6 — Guarantee
The Supplier guarantees the containers for a period of thirty-six (36) months from delivery.

## Article 7 — Amendments
Amendments to this Agreement shall be made in writing; verbal agreements are not binding.

## Article 8 — Applicable Law
The laws of the Federal Republic of Germany apply to this Agreement.
