# SUPPLY AND INSTALLATION AGREEMENT

Agreement No. SIA-2026-33

**Supplier:** Redwood Charging Infrastructure Inc.
**Customer:** Pacific Fleet Logistics Corp.

## 1. Scope
Supply and installation of sixteen (16) 150 kW DC fast chargers and one (1) 500 kWh buffer battery at Customer's Stockton depot.

## 2. Price
The total price is **USD 640,000.00**.

## 3. Payment
Customer will pay 20% on signing (Deposit), 50% on delivery of the chargers to site (Delivery), and 30% on energization of the final charger (Energization). Invoices are due **net 30 days**.

## 4. Completion
Energization shall be achieved no later than **31 October 2026**.

## 5. Liquidated Damages
0.25% of the total price per week of delay in energization, capped at 3% of the total price.

## 6. Warranty
Eighteen (18) months from energization.

## 7. Changes
Any change to scope or price requires a written amendment signed by both parties.

3. Section 4 (Completion) is amended so that energization shall be achieved no later than **15 December 2026**.
4. All other terms remain unchanged. The payment percentages in Section 3 apply to the amended total price.
