# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT — POWERWALL BACKUP UNITS

Agreement Reference: MSA-PB-0088

**Supplier:** Aurora Residential Power, LLC
**Customer:** Coastline Home Builders, Inc.

## 1. Products
Supplier will deliver and install 240 residential battery backup units (13.5 kWh each) across Customer's Seabreeze Estates development, in phases as scheduled in Exhibit B.

## 2. Pricing
The total fixed price for all 240 units including installation is **USD 1,200,000.00** (USD 5,000.00 per installed unit). No other charges apply.

## 3. Invoicing and Payment
Supplier may invoice **25% on contract signature**, **50% on completion of Phase 1 installation (120 units)**, and **25% on completion of Phase 2 installation (remaining 120 units)**. Invoices are payable **net 30 days**.

## 4. Schedule
Phase 2 installation shall be complete by **31 December 2026**.

## 5. Warranty
Supplier provides a **ten (10) year** manufacturer warranty on each unit, passed through from the manufacturer. Workmanship on installation is warranted for 24 months.

## 6. Order of Precedence
If this Agreement and any Exhibit conflict, this Agreement controls.

## 7. Changes
Change orders must be in writing and signed by both parties.
