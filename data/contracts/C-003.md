# SOLAR + STORAGE EPC SUBCONTRACT

Subcontract No. SC-2026-0419

Between **Bluewater Renewables Construction Co.** ("Contractor") and **Granite Ridge Electrical Services, Inc.** ("Subcontractor").

## 1. Work
Subcontractor shall furnish labor and materials for the DC collection system, battery enclosure foundations and medium-voltage interconnection for the Granite Ridge 12 MW solar-plus-storage project in Mohave County, Arizona.

## 2. Subcontract Sum
Contractor shall pay Subcontractor the fixed sum of **USD 915,000.00** (the "Subcontract Sum") for the complete Work.

## 3. Progress Payments
Subcontractor may invoice as follows: Mobilization — 15% of the Subcontract Sum upon mobilization to site; Rough-in Complete — 45% upon completion of underground conduit and foundations; Mechanical Completion — 30% upon Contractor's acceptance of mechanical completion; Retainage Release — 10% upon final acceptance by the Owner. Payment is due **net 60 days** from receipt of a correct invoice.

## 4. Schedule
Mechanical completion shall be achieved by **2026-11-20**.

## 5. Delay Damages
For each week that mechanical completion is delayed beyond the date in Section 4, Subcontractor shall pay Contractor delay damages of **1.0% of the Subcontract Sum**, capped at **10% of the Subcontract Sum**.

## 6. Warranty
Subcontractor warrants the Work for **twelve (12) months** after final acceptance.

## 7. Changes
Changes to the Work require a written change order executed by both parties before the changed work begins.
